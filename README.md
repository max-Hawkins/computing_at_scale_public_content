# Computing at Scale
**The Design, Operation, and Societal Impacts of Data Centers**

Welcome to the open-source curriculum for **Computing at Scale**, a course offered by Max Hawkins through Georgia Tech's **Online Masters of Computer Science (OMSCS)**.

This course aims to provide understanding of the details and context of computing at scale. AI datacenters are proliferating and causing many downstream impacts. How did we get here? What does a datacenter even look like? Why/when do they consume water/energy? What are the limits and implications of computing at scale?

I intend to cover the full stack of hyperscale computing: from **GPU architectures** and **thermal management** to **energy grid economics** and **ethics**.

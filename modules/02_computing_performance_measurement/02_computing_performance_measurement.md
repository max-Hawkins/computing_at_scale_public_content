## Activities
- Find a problem or deficiency in a computing performance unit, metric or benchmark. Identify it, explain why it's a problem, and provide at least one potential solution.

## Self-Evaluation
The following questions are meant for you to assess if you understand the material.

- What are common ways of measuring computing performance? What differentiates the variety of metrics?

- What is the difference between the Top/Graph/Green 500 lists? Why do you think these all exist?

- How do AI inference providers measure performance differently than traditional HPC (if at all)?

- What is the "Back to Bits" paper proposing? Why?

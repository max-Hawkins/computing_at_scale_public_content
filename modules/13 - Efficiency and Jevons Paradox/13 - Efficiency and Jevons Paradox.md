# Efficiency and Jevon's Paradox

## Content Overview
- What is Jevon's Paradox?
- Historical examples of the rebound effect
- Efficiency improvements in computing
- Increased efficiency lead to decreased or increased resource consumption?
- Implications for sustainable computing

## Relevant Media
- [Jevons paradox wiki page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jevons_paradox)

## Activities
- Research historical examples of Jevon's Paradox in different industries
- Analyze how computing efficiency improvements have affected total energy consumption
- Discuss: When are efficiency improvements good/bad for sustainability?

## Self-Evaluation
- What is Jevons paradox in your own words?
- How does Jevon's Paradox apply to computing at scale?
- Can you identify examples where efficiency improvements led to increased total consumption?
- What are the policy implications of Jevon's Paradox?
- How *should* we think about efficiency in the context of sustainability?
